<h1>Chocolate Sales Analysis using PowerBI and MS-EXCEL</h1>

<h2>Chocolate Sales Analysis project Powerbi Dashboard</h2>

<h3>Business Requirements</h3>

The Dessert Chocolate Company has undertaken a comprehensive data analysis project to gain insights into its sales performance, customer base, and shipment efficiency. The Power BI dashboard provides a detailed overview of key metrics and trends, enabling stakeholders to make informed decisions.

<h3>KPI's Requirements</h3>

1. <b>Total Sales:</b> The overall revenue generated from Shipment's sale.<br>
2. <b>Total Boxes:</b> Total no of boxes sent to the Shipment.<br>
3. <b>Total Shipments:</b> The total count of Shipment.<br>
4. <b>Total Costs:</b> Total Costs of Shipments.<br>
5. <b>Total Profits:</b> Total Profit.<br>

<b>Tools/Skills Used:</b>

2.	Bookmarks
3.	Conditional Formatting
4.	Dax Measures
5.	Dynamic Trend
6.	Dashboard Design
7.	Field Params
8.	Grouping
9.	Histogram Chart
10.	KPI Cards
11.	Reference Labels
12.	Tooltips
13.	Time Intelligence
14.	Table Design
15.	Zoom Slider

<h3>Insights</h3>

1. <b>Total Sales Performance:</b> The company achieved total sales of $34.04M, increased by 11.3% as compared to previous year. It reflecting strong market demand and effective sales strategies.
2. <b>Total Boxes:</b> $2M boxes successfully sent in Shipment. No of boxes increased by 3.9%.
3. <b>Total Shipments:</b> The company successfully managed 6,000 shipments, ensuring timely delivery to customers.
4. <b>Sales by Category:</b> The bar chart shows that ‘Bars’ are the top-selling category, followed by ‘Bites’ and ‘Others.’ This indicates a preference for traditional chocolate bars among customers.
5. <b>Shipments by Country:</b> The bar chart reveals that Australia has the highest number of shipments, followed by Canada, India, the UK, and the USA. This indicates strong international demand and efficient logistics operations.
6. <b>Achievements & Targets:</b> The circular gauge shows a 62.3% achievement towards the company’s target, indicating progress and areas for improvement. 

<h3>Key Learnings</h3>

1.	Learned about cleaning data.
2.	Learned about New Card Visual and New Slicer Visual.
3.	Learned about Zoom Slider and Gauze Visual.
4.	Learned about some Advanced DAX functions and measures.
5.	Learned about some major analytical visuals and reports.

<h3>Conclusion</h3>

The Power BI dashboard provides a comprehensive and visually appealing overview of the Awesome Chocolate Company’s performance. It highlights key metrics, sales trends, shipment efficiency, product performance, and cost, sales volume, and profit trends, enabling stakeholders to make data-driven decisions and strategize for future growth.
