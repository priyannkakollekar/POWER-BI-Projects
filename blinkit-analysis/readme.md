<h1>Blinkit Analysis using PowerBI and MS-EXCEL</h1>

<h2>Blinkit analysis project Powerbi Dashboard</h2>

<h3>Business Requirements</h3>

To conduct a comprehensive analysis of Blinkit's sales performance, customer satisfaction, and inventory distribution to identify key insights and opportunities for optimization using various KPIs and visualizations in Power BI.

<h3>KPI's Requirements</h3>

1. <b>Total Sales:</b> The overall revenue generated from all items sold.<br>
2. <b>Average Sales:</b> The average revenue per sale.<br>
3. <b>Number of Items:</b> The total count of different items sold.<br>
4. <b>Average Rating:</b> The average customer rating for items sold.<br>

<h3>Chart's Requirements</h3>

1. <b>Total Sales by Fat Content:</b><br>
Objective: Analyze the impact of fat content on total sales.<br>
Additional KPI Metrics: Assess how other KPIs (Average Sales, Number of Items, Average Rating) vary with fat content.<br>
Chart Type: Donut Chart.

2. <b>Total Sales by Item Type:</b><br>
Objective: Identify the performance of different item types in terms of total sales.<br>
Additional KPI Metrics: Assess how other KPIs (Average Sales, Number of Items, Average Rating) vary with fat content.<br>
Chart Type: Bar Chart.

3. <b>Fat Content by Outlet for Total Sales:</b><br>
Objective: Compare total sales across different outlets segmented by fat content.<br>
Additional KPI Metrics: Assess how other KPIs (Average Sales, Number of Items, Average Rating) vary with fat content.<br>
Chart Type: Stacked Column Chart.

4. <b>Total Sales by Outlet Establishment:</b><br>
Objective: Evaluate how the age or type of outlet establishment influences total sales.<br>
Chart Type: Line Chart.

5. <b>Sales by Outlet Size:</b><br>
Objective: Analyze the correlation between outlet size and total sales.<br>
Chart Type: Donut/Pie Chart.

6. <b>Sales by Outlet Location:</b><br>
Objective: Assess the geographic distribution of sales across different locations.<br>
Chart Type: Funnel Map.

7. <b>All Metrics by Outlet Type:</b><br>
Objective: Provide a comprehensive view of all key metrics (Total Sales, Average Sales, Number of Items, Average Rating) broken down by different outlet types.<br>
Chart Type: Matrix Card.

<h3>Insights</h3>

1. <b>Total Sales Performance:</b> Blinkit recorded total sales of ₹1.2M, with an average sales value of ₹141 per transaction. This indicates a decent volume of transactions with an acceptable average value, reflecting a steady flow of sales activity.
2. <b>Customer Satisfaction:</b> The average customer rating of 4 out of 5 across 8,523 transactions suggests that customers are generally satisfied with the service provided by Blinkit. Maintaining a high customer rating is crucial for long-term success and customer retention.
3. <b>Product Segmentation:</b> Sales are broken down by fat content, with ₹776.3K coming from low-fat items and ₹425.4K from regular-fat items. This breakdown can help in understanding customer preferences and optimizing the product offerings accordingly.
4. <b>Outlet Analysis:</b> Sales by outlet tier reveal that Tier 3 outlets generated ₹0.31M, with Tier 2 and Tier 1 outlets following in sales performance. Understanding the sales distribution across different outlet tiers can aid in strategic decision-making and resource allocation.
5. <b>Item Type Sales:</b> Sales by item type highlight categories such as fruits, snacks, and household items. Analyzing sales by item type can help in identifying popular product categories and optimizing inventory and marketing strategies accordingly.
6. <b>Establishment Year Analysis:</b> Consistent sales performance from 2012 to 2022 indicates the sustainability and growth of Blinkit over the years. Understanding sales trends over different establishment years can provide insights into the company's historical performance and growth trajectory.
7. <b>Outlet Size and Type:</b> Medium-sized outlets generated the highest sales at ₹507.9K, while supermarkets (Type 1) recorded the highest sales value at ₹7,87,549.9. Analyzing sales by outlet size and type can help in optimizing the outlet network and focusing on high-performing locations.

Overall, the comprehensive sales performance dashboard for Blinkit provides valuable insights into key metrics such as sales distribution, customer satisfaction, product segmentation, outlet analysis, and historical performance. These insights can guide strategic decision-making, marketing strategies, and operational improvements to drive further growth and success for Blinkit.
